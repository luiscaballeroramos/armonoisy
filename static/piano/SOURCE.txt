The four WAVs are redistributed unmodified. Their labels C3, C4, C5 and C6
sound approximately at C4, C5, C6 and C7 respectively. The player selects the
nearest sample and corrects its measured frequency to the score pitch.
Piano samples are not looped: their recorded sound decays naturally.
