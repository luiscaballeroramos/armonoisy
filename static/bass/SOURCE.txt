The FLAC recordings in samples/finger are redistributed unmodified, with names
E.flac, A.flac, C.flac and D#.flac changed to E1.flac, A1.flac, C2.flac and
Dsharp2.flac. The player corrects their measured frequencies and transposes
them to the score melody three octaves lower. Samples are not looped.
